Download for Mac Chrome Extension

After you download

  1. Drag usemac to Applications and open it.
  2. Allow Accessibility and Screen Recording when macOS asks.
  3. Add the Chrome extension: open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and choose /Applications/usemac.app/Contents/Resources/chrome-extension. Then paste the pairing key from usemac → Settings → General → Chrome Extension.
  4. Paste the setup prompt into your agent.
