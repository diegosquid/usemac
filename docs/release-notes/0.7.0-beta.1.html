What's New in UseMac 0.7.0 Beta 1

  • A live preview that moves on its own: with several agents at once, their cards stack behind the front one and take turns in front every few seconds. Hover the preview to hold the current card.
  • Each task has its own soft color tag in the preview, so two sessions of the same agent are easy to tell apart.
  • The preview keeps one size: the page fills it from the top-left corner and the rest is cut off.
  • Agents never resize your Chrome tabs any more: every tab keeps its real size.
  • The preview only shows what is happening: stop and resume from the UseMac menu or with ⌘⇧⎋.
  • A lighter, softer shadow around the preview.

After updating, reload the UseMac Browser Bridge from Settings → Chrome Extension.

This is a beta. Please report problems at github.com/diegosquid/usemac/issues, or ask your agent to prepare a report.
